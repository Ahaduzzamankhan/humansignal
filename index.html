01 Diagnosis

The sentence tells you what’s wrong.

Three signals, one sentence. Each is a prompt to inspect, not a verdict about authorship.

Revised

Three of our four payment providers retire their legacy APIs this year, so checkout needs a migration plan. We can migrate one provider at a time behind a feature flag.

Illustrative revision. The original makes no checkable claim, so the rewrite commits to facts the writer must verify — not facts the editor may invent.

03 Method

Read first. Edit last.

Most bad editing happens during the first read. The workflow separates diagnosis from revision and makes preservation a checked step rather than a hope.

Steps 01–05

Read

  • Read the whole text once, without editing.
  • State its claim or function in one sentence.
  • Identify audience, channel, and consequence.
  • Choose the writing category and mode.
  • Record the existing author voice.
Steps 06–12

Diagnose

  • Vocabulary: precision, repetition, register.
  • Sentences: clause logic, voice, rhythm.
  • Paragraphs: jobs, boundaries, progression.
  • Repetition, vague claims, redundant explanation.
  • Assign severity by reader effect.
Step 13

Edit

  • Remove filler and redundancy first.
  • Then fix facts and terminology.
  • Then structure, then vocabulary.
  • Consider rhythm only at the end.
  • Name the problem before changing a sentence.
Steps 14–16

Verify

  • Match every original claim to its revision.
  • Check the reverse: no new claims.
  • Diff numbers, units, conditions, citations.
  • Confirm register, stance, and voice.
  • Run the quality checklist.

04 Writing modes

Five registers. One standard of care.

The mode changes the register, density, and conventions. It does not change the rule that meaning survives the edit.

01

Academic

Evidence outranks elegance

Essays, papers, literature reviews, theses, and critical analysis. Attribution is explicit, confidence is calibrated, and the verb matches the design of the study.

“The effect is consistent with the model, though the sample cannot separate the two mechanisms.” Academic register
02

Technical

Accuracy outranks style

READMEs, API references, guides, changelogs, and error messages. Identifiers are data, conditions come before consequences, and every instruction has a testable outcome.

“The endpoint returns 404 when the job does not exist.” Technical register
03

Conversational

Register fit outranks completeness

Chat, short email, support replies, and forum posts. Warmth is optional; clarity is not. Ceremony is replaced by a direct question and a real deadline.

“Any update on the migration? I need to tell the vendor by Thursday.” Conversational register
04

Professional

Decisions outrank ceremony

Memos, proposals, status updates, feedback, policy, and client correspondence. The reader should finish knowing what was decided, by whom, and what happens next.

“Decision needed by Friday: renew the contract or renegotiate the SLA.” Professional register
05

Creative

Voice outranks density

Fiction, narrative nonfiction, personal essays, and scripts. Deliberate fragments, dashes, and rhythm are devices, not defects, and the edit protects the effect.

“The rain had stopped, but the roof still ticked.” Creative register

05 Worked examples

The same method, four genres.

Seventy worked examples sit in the repository, each with seven parts. These four show the range: the diagnosis, the revision, and what happened to the meaning.

OriginalGEN-01

“In today’s rapidly evolving digital landscape, businesses must adapt or risk falling behind.”

Formulaic framingFalse dilemmaNo specific subject
RevisedIllustrative

“Three of our four payment providers will retire their legacy APIs this year, so checkout needs a migration plan.”

Dated conditionConcrete stakesNarrower scope

Diagnosis

The sentence pairs an abstract time frame with a binary threat. Neither term is defined, and the claim would fit any business document.

Improvement

A dated, specific condition replaces the trend claim. The stakes become concrete enough to act on.

Meaning

Narrower scope. The original’s general claim would require evidence and a defined audience; the revision does not overreach.

OriginalACA-01

“In recent years, artificial intelligence has become increasingly important in education, and researchers around the world have paid growing attention to its potential.”

Importance assertionUnnamed consensusNo research question
RevisedSource required

“Automated essay scoring is now used in state testing programs, yet the score agreement between systems and human raters varies widely across student populations [source]. This study asks whether that variation tracks differences in writing prompt genre.”

Attributable practiceSpecific gapExplicit question

Diagnosis

Three trends, no specifics: a time frame, a field, and a group. The paragraph could introduce any paper on the topic.

Improvement

A dated practice replaces “increasingly important”; a documented gap replaces “growing attention.”

Meaning

Narrower scope. The writer must supply a verified source and confirm that the variation is the gap under study.

OriginalTEC-01

“Welcome to Project Atlas, a revolutionary, next-generation platform that seamlessly empowers developers to unlock the full potential of their workflows.”

Promotional modifiersUndefined termsNo function stated
RevisedVerify behavior

“Atlas is a command-line build cache for monorepos. It stores compiled artifacts locally and on a shared remote, so a CI job can reuse output from an earlier commit.”

CategoryMechanismBenefit

Diagnosis

The sentence names the project and describes it entirely in evaluative terms. A reader cannot tell whether it is a build tool, a database, or a dashboard.

Improvement

Two sentences state what it is, how it works, and why that matters to a CI job.

Meaning

Changed, by necessity. The original made no falsifiable claim; the revision supplies one that must match the project’s actual behavior.

OriginalCON-01

“I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to respectfully request that you provide me with an update regarding the status of the migration project at your earliest convenience.”

Ceremonial framesPassive indirectionNo deadline
RevisedPeer register

“Any update on the migration? I need to tell the vendor by Thursday whether the cutover is still on for the 12th.”

Question firstExplicit deadlineReason given

Diagnosis

One request, three sentences of ceremony, and a deadline left to the reader’s interpretation.

Improvement

The ask comes first, the deadline is explicit, and the reason makes it easy to answer.

Meaning

Preserved. The request is unchanged; the social distance is reduced, which fits a colleague relationship.

06 Quality assurance

Nothing ships on vibe.

A revision is checked against claims, conditions, numbers, citations, register, and voice. The checklist is boring on purpose.

Meaning preservation

Claims, intent, technical detail, qualifications, citations, numbers, and context — matched claim by claim, plus a reverse check for anything the edit quietly added.

Failure-mode tests

Regression cases

Over-editing, technical drift, synonym rotation, register changes in both directions, invented specificity, voice flattening, and conclusions the evidence does not support.

Release threshold

A critical factual failure stops delivery. More than two failures in expression, clarity, or structure means revise before sending. Any voice failure means re-check whether the edit flattened the author.

07 Limits

What it refuses to do.

“A detector score is not evidence of authorship.”

Detectors estimate statistical properties of a sample. They do not observe how a text was produced. Human and machine writing overlap on every measured feature, and paraphrasing can move a score without changing whose words these are. Read the technical case ↗

×

Detector evasion

No paraphrasing procedures whose purpose is to defeat a classifier. If asked to make text “undetectable,” the skill explains the limits and offers a quality edit instead.

×

Fabrication

No invented sources, findings, quotes, credentials, measurements, or experience. Where specificity is missing, the gap is flagged — never filled with a plausible number.

×

False authority

It can make a legal, medical, or financial text clearer. It cannot certify that text as correct. Domain expertise is not a style problem.

×

Uniformity

No fixed 120-point checklist applied to every document. Presence is not guilt, and a deliberate fragment in an essay is not the same defect as a fragment in a contract.

08 Use it

Drop it into a skills directory.

Plain Markdown, no build step, no dependencies. The mode and the reference files travel with SKILL.md, so every relative link resolves.

  1. Copy the folder

    Keep SKILL.md at the root and the references/, modes/, examples/, and tests/ directories beside it.

  2. Check the frontmatter

    The skill declares name: humansignal and version: 2.1.0. No configuration or API key is required.

  3. Ask for an edit — or an audit

    Name the reader and the purpose, or name the mode explicitly. Ask for the diagnosis without a rewrite if you want to review the findings first.
